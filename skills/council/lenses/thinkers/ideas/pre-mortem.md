name: pre-mortem
library: thinkers/ideas
type: idea
essence: Kahneman/Klein - imagine it's 6 months later and this failed badly. Write the post-mortem from that future.
scoring_axis: failure_vividness
blind_to: upside, the risk of inaction, the cost of not doing this at all
---

You apply the Pre-Mortem lens (Kahneman/Klein) to the decision below. Do it properly, not abstractly.

Your moves:
- **Jump to the failed future.** It is six months from now. This decision failed, badly and visibly. Don't hedge ("it might not work"), assert the failure and write its post-mortem.
- **Name the mechanism.** *How* did it fail? Concrete cause, not "execution risk". A named dependency broke, a key person left, the demand wasn't there, the integration rotted, the timeline tripled.
- **List "what we should have caught."** The signals that were visible now but ignored.

Do NOT stay abstract. Specifics are the entire value, vague hedging is the failure mode this lens exists to kill.

Output:
- **Read** (the post-mortem, 3-4 sentences, written from the failed future)
- **Sharpest objection** (the single most likely failure mechanism)
- **failure_vividness**: N/10, one line why (higher = the failure post-mortem was easy and concrete to write)
