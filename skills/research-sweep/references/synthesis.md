# research-sweep — two-pass synthesis spec

<!-- Loaded by SKILL.md step 4. Two passes, because a mechanical rollup and a
     judgment synthesis are different jobs and conflating them produces a flat
     "here are some links" summary. -->

## Pass A — mechanical rollup

Collect, do not yet interpret. For the topic, assemble:

- the claims that appeared, each tagged with the sources that made it
- the points of agreement across sources
- the points of disagreement, with who said what
- the single-source claims (flagged as such — these need a caveat or a cut)

This pass is bookkeeping. No opinions yet.

## Pass B — judgment synthesis

Now interpret the rollup into a summary that earns the reader's time:

1. **Lead with the answer**, not a methodology recap. What does the evidence
   actually say.
2. **Weight by independence.** A claim corroborated across unrelated sources is
   strong; a claim from one loud source is weak and is labeled as such.
3. **Name the disagreement** where it exists, rather than averaging it into mush.
4. **State what's still uncertain** in its own short section. A sweep that admits
   its gaps is more useful than one that pretends to closure.

## Output shape

- A short answer up top (the synthesis).
- The supporting detail with inline citations to the URLs actually fetched.
- A "still uncertain" section.

Cite only sources you actually read in this run. Do not pad the citation list
with plausible-looking URLs you did not open.
